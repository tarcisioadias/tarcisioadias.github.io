Research

My research investigates how syntactic structure and morphological composition interact with cross-linguistic variation. I work primarily in generative syntax and morphology, with particular attention to Brazilian Portuguese and Chichewa.

Research area

Syntax

Subjecthood, hyper-raising, A/A′ dependencies, locality, and cross-linguistic variation.
