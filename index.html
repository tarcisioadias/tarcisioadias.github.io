Generative syntax · Morphology · Cross-linguistic variation

Tarcisio A. Dias

PhD Candidate in Linguistics at the University of Connecticut

I am a generative syntactician and morphologist. My research examines subjecthood, movement, and word structure across languages and what this variation reveals about grammatical theory. My dissertation, Edge Subjects, develops a cross-linguistic account of hyper-raising, drawing primarily on Brazilian Portuguese and Chichewa.
