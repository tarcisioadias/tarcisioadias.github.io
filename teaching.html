Teaching Experience

My teaching aims to make linguistic theory accessible through problem-solving, comparative language data, and explicit argumentation. I encourage students to treat their linguistic intuitions as evidence that can be examined, tested, and developed into hypotheses about the structure of language.

In introductory courses, I use collaborative and data-driven activities to help students distinguish linguistic knowledge from prescriptive rules and develop an understanding of language as a complex cognitive system. In syntax courses, I emphasize the connection between empirical observations, structural representations, and theoretical analysis.
