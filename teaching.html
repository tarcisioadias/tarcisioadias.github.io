Teaching

University of Connecticut 🇺🇸

Instructor of record for LING3610W Language and Culture (Writing Competency), Fall 2024.

Topics include the evolution of the human language capacity; the principles of historical language change including reconstruction of Indo-European and Native American language families; writing systems; linguistic forms such as Pidgins and Creoles arising from languages in contact; the interaction between language and political systems, the struggle for human rights, gender, ethnicity, and ethnobiology.

Instructor of record for LING2010Q The Science of Linguistics (Quantitave Competency), Fall 2023, Fall 2021.

An introduction to the methods and major findings of linguistic research as applied to the sound systems of languages and the structure and meaning of words and sentences.

Instructor of record for LING1010 Language and Mind, Summer 2022.

Discussion of nature-nurture debate with specific reference to language acquisition. Pros and cons of Chomsky's Innateness Hypothesis.

Discussion Section Leader for LING1010 Language and Mind, Fall 2026, Fall 2025, Spring 2023 (Instructor: Jon Gajewski) Spring 2026 (Magdalena Kaufman) Spring 2024, Fall 2022 (Harry van der Hulst)

TA for COGS 2201 Foundations of Cognitive Science, Spring 2022 (Instructors: Rachel Theodore, William Snyder)

Origins of and current developments within scientific study of the mind-brain. Topics include: computational theories of mind, artificial and natural intelligence, cognitive neuroscience and the mind/body problem, embodied and distributed cognition, neural networks, self-organizing cognitive systems, learning and innateness.
