About

Research interests

I am a generative syntactician and morphologist interested in how cross-linguistic variation can inform general theories of grammatical structure. My research examines subjecthood, movement, locality, and word formation, with particular attention to Brazilian Portuguese and Chichewa.

My dissertation, Edge Subjects, investigates hyper-raising and the syntactic positions available to subjects. It develops an account in which hyper-raising follows from independently motivated properties of subject positions, rather than from defective or exceptional functional heads. My other research includes work on wh-movement and on the interaction of evaluative morphology, gender, and theme vowels in Brazilian Portuguese.

More information about these projects is available on my Research page.
